Use the standards of linguistics. Identify language varieties, speaker population, corpus or elicitation setting, units of analysis, representation, grammar or processing claim, and comparison class. Check glosses and annotation, acceptability design, inter-speaker variation, data provenance, descriptive adequacy, typological sampling, and whether the analysis separates competence, processing, frequency, discourse, and social context.

Assess whether proposed generalizations survive counterexamples, alternations, and relevant cross-linguistic evidence. Examine theory-dependent coding, segmentation and alignment, translation, diachronic direction, and the relation between model performance and linguistic explanation. Require formal representations to generate the claimed contrasts and empirical data to discriminate among analyses.
