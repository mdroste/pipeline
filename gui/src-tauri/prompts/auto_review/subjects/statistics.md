Use the standards of mathematical and applied statistics. Identify the statistical experiment, estimand or prediction target, sampling mechanism, information set, loss, and uncertainty object. Check identifiability before estimation, the distinction between conditional and marginal claims, finite-sample versus asymptotic guarantees, regularity and positivity conditions, and whether tuning or model selection is included in reported uncertainty.

Evaluate robustness to misspecification, dependence, missingness, measurement error, multiplicity, and distribution shift when relevant. Separate mathematical guarantees from empirical benchmark performance. Determine whether simulation and application designs actually probe the regime covered by the claim, including weak signals, boundary cases, nonregularity, and realistic sample sizes.
