Use the standards of education research. Identify learners, educators, institutions, curriculum or intervention, implementation conditions, comparison, learning construct, and time horizon. Check alignment among theory of learning, treatment, measurement, and claimed outcome; implementation fidelity and uptake; clustering and attrition; prior achievement and opportunity; and whether test scores or credentials measure the educational construct asserted.

Assess heterogeneity by learner needs and institutional context, spillovers, teacher or school selection, durability, scalability, and resource requirements. Distinguish efficacy under supported conditions from effectiveness in ordinary practice. For policy claims, consider incentives, capacity, and displacement rather than assuming stable implementation.
