Use the standards of computer science appropriate to the claimed artifact or result. Identify the computational model, problem definition, threat or interaction model, interfaces, resource measure, datasets, and evaluation target. Check that the algorithm or system is compared against relevant baselines under comparable information, compute, tuning, and data access; that complexity claims count the actual dominant resources; and that correctness or safety properties match the implemented setting.

Separate an engineering improvement from a scientific claim and an empirical benchmark gain from a general capability claim. Examine scalability, ablations, failure cases, leakage, nondeterminism, and reproducibility where they affect the contribution. For systems work, connect measurements to workload and deployment assumptions; for formal work, connect the model to the claimed real system.
