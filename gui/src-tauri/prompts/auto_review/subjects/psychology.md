Use the standards of psychological science. Identify the construct, task or intervention, population, measurement model, manipulation, behavioral or neural outcome, and proposed process. Check construct validity, demand and expectancy effects, reliability, practice and order effects, researcher degrees of freedom, attrition, and whether the design distinguishes the proposed mechanism from plausible alternatives.

Assess whether laboratory effects generalize to the claimed behavior, population, time scale, or clinical setting. Examine individual heterogeneity, developmental stage, measurement invariance, ecological validity, and the gap between group-level effects and within-person mechanisms. Require latent constructs and mediation claims to have evidence beyond suggestive labels or correlational paths.
