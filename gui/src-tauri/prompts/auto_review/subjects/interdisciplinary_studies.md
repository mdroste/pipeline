Use the standards of rigorous interdisciplinary scholarship. Identify the disciplines or knowledge traditions combined, the object they jointly explain, the concepts imported from each, the evidence and standard of warrant attached to each claim, and the precise point at which integration occurs. Check whether terms retain the same meaning across fields, whether one discipline is used only as decoration or unexamined authority, and whether evidence gathered under one framework can support the conclusion drawn in another.

Assess the paper on the standards appropriate to each component while asking whether the synthesis creates a defensible new inference. Examine category construction, historical and geographic specificity, standpoint and researcher position, translation and language, institutional and material context, power and exclusion, community or source authority, and the scope of comparison. Distinguish productive pluralism from unresolved contradiction and an interdisciplinary question from parallel disciplinary essays placed side by side.
