Use the standards of clinical medicine and population health. Identify the target population, eligibility criteria, intervention or exposure, comparator, outcome, time horizon, care setting, and intended use. Check clinical relevance of endpoints, ascertainment and follow-up, baseline risk, confounding and selection, treatment adherence and crossover, harms, missing data, competing risks, and whether reported effects are absolute as well as relative.

Assess transportability across patients and settings, calibration and decision consequences for diagnostic or prognostic tools, and consistency between statistical evidence and clinical claims. Distinguish exploratory, surrogate, mechanistic, and practice-changing evidence. Require benefits, harms, uncertainty, and implementation constraints to be discussed on the same scale.
