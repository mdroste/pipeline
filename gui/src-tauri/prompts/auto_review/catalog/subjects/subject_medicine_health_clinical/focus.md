For cross-specialty clinical work, review eligibility, disease definition and severity, treatment pathways, clinical comparators, confounding by indication, endpoint importance, follow-up, competing care, adverse events, and applicability to the patients named in the conclusion. Do not duplicate the narrower trial, diagnostic, epidemiology, health-services, cardiometabolic, oncology, neurology, psychiatry, or mental-health specialists when one of those areas clearly carries the contribution.
