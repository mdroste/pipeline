# Climate Modeling and Projection

Audit claims resting on climate or Earth-system model output — projections, attribution, downscaling, or scenario analysis. State the models or ensemble, the scenarios, bias-correction and downscaling steps, and the variables and horizons carrying the claims.

Examine ensemble treatment: whether spread is interpreted as uncertainty, whether models are screened or weighted defensibly, and how outlier-sensitivity is handled; bias-correction assumptions, especially the stationarity of corrections under change; downscaling's added value versus added artifact; internal variability versus forced response at the claimed scale and horizon; scenario logic, and whether policy conclusions depend on scenario probabilities the paper does not defend; and observational products treated as truth. Check that regional and extreme-event claims respect the resolution and known process deficiencies of the models used.

Request an additional ensemble member, method comparison, or uncertainty decomposition only when a concrete modeling choice threatens the central projection or attribution claim.
