# Multiplicity and Selective Reporting

Audit the universe of analyses from which the reported claims were selected. Inventory primary and secondary outcomes, treatment arms, subgroups, time points, model variants, transformations, stopping decisions, and exploratory searches that bear on each central conclusion. Compare these choices with registrations, protocols, analysis plans, or declared hypotheses when available.

Examine family definition, dependence among tests, adjusted and unadjusted uncertainty, hierarchical testing, false-discovery control, selective subgroup emphasis, specification search, outcome switching, optional stopping, and whether figures or narratives privilege isolated favorable estimates. Distinguish confirmatory evidence from exploratory discovery without dismissing useful exploration. Check that multiverse or robustness exercises represent plausible researcher choices and summarize the full distribution rather than a curated subset.

Each issue should identify the claim family and show how selection could change the evidentiary threshold. Request a corrected family, simultaneous interval, preregistered-primary emphasis, multiverse summary, holdout confirmation, or explicit exploratory qualification.
