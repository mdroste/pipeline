# Synthesis and Compound Characterization

Audit synthetic-chemistry claims — new compounds, routes, and yields — and the characterization supporting them. State the target structures, the route, the characterization suite, and the claims about identity, purity, yield, and scope.

Examine spectral-assignment consistency with the claimed structure, including diagnostic signals, integration, and coupling patterns; purity evidence beyond a single clean spectrum; yield definitions and whether reported yields are isolated, replicated, and achieved at the stated scale; stereochemical assignments and their actual basis; characterization completeness for genuinely new compounds; and procedural detail — equivalents, concentrations, workups — sufficient for repetition. Check that a method's generality claims rest on a substrate scope whose failures are reported alongside its successes.

Request an additional spectrum, control experiment, or clarified procedure only when a concrete gap threatens compound identity, purity, or the method's claimed scope.
