# Simulation and Numerical Evidence

Audit claims supported by Monte Carlo experiments, numerical examples, synthetic data, computational experiments, or approximation benchmarks. Check the data-generating process, parameter range, baselines, random seeds and replication count, convergence, numerical precision, coverage of adverse cases, and whether performance metrics answer the stated question.

Look for tuning on evaluation cases, incomparable computational budgets, omitted failure rates, unstable tails, insufficient repetitions, and conclusions extrapolated beyond the simulated region. Distinguish evidence that an algorithm works in the chosen design from a theorem or general empirical claim.

Each issue must identify the experiment, figure, table, or algorithm affected and propose a concrete additional design, benchmark, convergence check, or qualification. Report at most seven issues.
