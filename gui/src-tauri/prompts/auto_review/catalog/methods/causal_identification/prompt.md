# Observational Causal Identification

Audit the paper's observational causal claims. For each central estimate, state the estimand, treatment or exposure, identifying variation, comparison group, timing, and assumptions that map the estimate to the claimed causal object.

Name setting-specific threats rather than generic endogeneity. Explain the causal chain, what is correlated with what, and the direction of bias when it is determined; when competing channels make the sign ambiguous, identify them. Check design-specific requirements such as parallel trends and treatment timing, exclusion and monotonicity, continuity and manipulation, selection, spillovers, clustering, serial or spatial dependence, and post-treatment controls. Judge existing diagnostics before asking for another robustness check.

Every comment must cite the relevant table, figure, specification, or design passage and propose a feasible diagnostic, alternative specification, bound, or narrower interpretation. Do not review descriptive results that make no causal claim. Report at most seven issues.
