# Mediation and Mechanism Analysis

Audit claims about how an effect operates. State the treatment, mediator, outcome, timing, target direct or indirect effect, and causal model that connects them. Distinguish causal mediation from descriptive decomposition, attenuation after adding a variable, heterogeneous treatment effects, and evidence that an intermediate outcome merely moved.

Check treatment-mediator and mediator-outcome confounding, post-treatment confounders, exposure-induced selection, measurement error, treatment-mediator interaction, multiple or competing mediators, interference, and whether the mediator is manipulable at the level assumed. For mechanism experiments, determine which link in the chain is randomized and which remains observational. For structural or principal-stratification approaches, inspect cross-world or exclusion assumptions and whether sensitivity analysis covers plausible violations.

Do not accept coefficient changes as mechanism evidence by themselves. Each comment should specify the broken link and request the smallest design, sensitivity analysis, temporal test, intervention on the mediator, or qualification needed for the mechanism claim.
