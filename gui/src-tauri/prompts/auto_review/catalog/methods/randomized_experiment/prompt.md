# Randomized Experiment Audit

Audit whether random assignment and implementation support the experimental claims. Check the unit and mechanism of assignment, stratification or rerandomization, treatment delivery, noncompliance, attrition, interference, blinding, outcome construction, preregistration, multiple testing, stopping rules, clustering, and treatment-effect estimand.

Separate failures of randomization from failures of interpretation or external validity. Use balance tests appropriately: do not infer invalid randomization from one chance imbalance, but examine whether analysis conditions on post-randomization variables or departs selectively from the announced design. Check whether standard errors match the assignment level and whether subgroup or mechanism claims were powered and specified.

For each issue, cite the protocol, design section, flow diagram, or result table; explain the estimand consequence; and propose a concrete reanalysis or qualification. Do not import observational-identification objections that randomization resolves. Report at most seven issues.
