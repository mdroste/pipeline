# Crystallography and Structure Determination

Audit structural claims from diffraction or cryo-EM — small-molecule and macromolecular structures and the mechanistic conclusions drawn from them. State the technique, resolution, refinement statistics, deposition status, and which structural features carry the paper's claims.

Examine data-quality and refinement indicators appropriate to the technique and resolution; whether the features interpreted — ligands, waters, conformations, occupancies — are supported by density at the stated resolution; model bias in difference maps and ligand placement; disorder treatment and alternative conformations; geometry outliers concentrated at the mechanistically important site; and consistency between the static structure and the dynamic mechanism claimed. Check that deposition identifiers exist for the structures on which conclusions rest.

Request a density figure, revised refinement, or deposited data only when a concrete map-model gap threatens the structural feature the conclusion needs.
