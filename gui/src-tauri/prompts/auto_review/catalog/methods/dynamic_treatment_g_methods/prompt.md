# Time-Varying Treatments and G-Methods

Audit causal claims about treatment strategies sustained or varied over time — marginal structural models, the g-formula, structural nested models, or per-protocol trial emulations. State the treatment regimes compared, the time-varying confounders, and whether confounders affected by prior treatment make standard adjustment invalid.

Examine the sequential exchangeability argument at each decision time; positivity across strata and time, including nonoverlap concealed by weight truncation; weight construction and diagnostics for extreme weights; treatment-confounder feedback and whether conventional regression would have sufficed; alignment of time zero, eligibility, and strategy assignment against immortal-time and selection bias; and the handling of censoring. Check that the estimand — a specific regime contrast — is stated and that the emulated protocol is explicit enough to audit.

Require an alternative weighting, parametrization, or negative-control analysis only when a concrete violation would change the regime comparison. Do not demand g-methods where treatment is effectively point-in-time.
