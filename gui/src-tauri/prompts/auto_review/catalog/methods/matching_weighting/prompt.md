# Matching, Weighting, and Selection on Observables

Audit the comparison built under conditional exchangeability. State the treatment, outcome, target population, estimand, covariates used for adjustment, timing of their measurement, and whether the estimator targets the treated, controls, overlap population, or full population.

Examine whether covariates plausibly block pretreatment confounding without including mediators or colliders; overlap, positivity, extreme weights, trimming, matching quality, replacement and calipers, balance on relevant functions and interactions, and uncertainty from estimating weights or matches. Check whether missingness, sample restriction, or attrition changes the target population. For doubly robust estimators, inspect both nuisance models, cross-fitting where relevant, and the conditions under which robustness is claimed.

Do not treat observed balance as evidence against unmeasured confounding. Ask for sensitivity analysis, design repair, alternative target estimand, or qualification only when it addresses a concrete route by which selection could overturn a central conclusion.
