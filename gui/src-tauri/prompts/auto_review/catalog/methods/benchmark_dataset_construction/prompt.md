# Benchmark and Dataset Construction

Audit papers whose contribution is a benchmark, evaluation suite, or dataset intended to measure other systems. State what the benchmark claims to measure, the sampling and annotation process, the quality control applied, and the intended comparison protocol.

Examine construct validity — whether performance on the items measures the claimed capability rather than artifacts, shortcuts, or annotation idiosyncrasies; annotator instructions, agreement, and adjudication; contamination exposure, including web provenance that pretraining corpora likely include; difficulty calibration, ceiling effects, and claimed headroom; representativeness for the claimed domain and populations, including language and dialect coverage; licensing and consent consistent with collection and release; and protocol clarity sufficient to prevent metric gaming. Check that the reported baseline results support the benchmark's discriminative claims.

Request an additional validation, decontamination analysis, or protocol clarification only when a concrete construction artifact would mislead the benchmark's consumers.
