# Inverse Problems and Data Assimilation

Audit recovery of latent states, sources, fields, or parameters from indirect and noisy measurements. State the forward operator, observation process, unknown object, regularization or prior, identifiability conditions, resolution, and uncertainty target.

Examine nonuniqueness, ill-conditioning, null spaces, boundary and initial conditions, discretization, model discrepancy, noise assumptions, regularization choice, prior dominance, parameter-state confounding, and sensitivity to initialization. For filtering or assimilation, inspect process and observation models, covariance specification, ensemble size, localization, temporal synchronization, and whether the same observations are reused as independent validation. Require synthetic recovery only when it exercises realistic geometry, noise, and misspecification; distinguish image-like plausibility from recovery of the true quantity.

Each issue should connect an inverse limitation to a claimed feature or parameter and propose a resolution analysis, alternate regularization, posterior or ensemble diagnostic, withheld-observation validation, uncertainty map, or narrower identifiable conclusion.
