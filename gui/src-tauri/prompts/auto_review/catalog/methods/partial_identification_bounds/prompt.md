# Partial Identification and Bounds

Audit conclusions framed as bounds, breakdown points, or sensitivity regions — Manski-style bounds, Rosenbaum sensitivity analysis, selection-adjustment benchmarks, and related partial-identification methods. State the identified set's assumptions, the reported region, and the decision the bounds are meant to support.

Examine whether the maintained assumptions that narrow the bounds are defended rather than chosen for narrowness; the credibility ordering between the point-identifying assumptions rejected and the bounding assumptions kept; inference on the identified set versus the parameter; breakdown analyses whose benchmark — such as selection proportional to observables — is treated as a law rather than a convention; and whether the conclusion quietly reverts to a point estimate the bounds do not support. Check that wide or empty regions are reported as findings, not failures to be patched.

Request a different sensitivity parametrization or additional bounding information only when it would discriminate between conclusions the current region leaves open.
