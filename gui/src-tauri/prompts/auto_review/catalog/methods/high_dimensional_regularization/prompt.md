# High-Dimensional Estimation and Regularization

Audit claims based on many covariates, features, parameters, or candidate models. State the target—prediction, support recovery, nuisance adjustment, treatment effect, or scientific interpretation—the dimensional regime, regularizer, tuning procedure, and sample splitting or cross-fitting scheme.

Examine sparsity or structural assumptions, feature scaling, correlated predictors, tuning leakage, stability of selected variables, effective degrees of freedom, overlap, regularization bias, post-selection inference, and whether uncertainty accounts for model search. For double or debiased machine learning, inspect nuisance rates, orthogonality, fold construction, cluster structure, and repeated-split stability. For penalized prediction, require honest evaluation and distinguish predictive importance from causal or mechanistic importance.

Do not object merely because the model is complex. Each issue should identify a violated high-dimensional requirement and propose a nested validation design, stability analysis, alternative penalty, debiased estimator, simultaneous uncertainty statement, or narrower interpretation of selected features.
