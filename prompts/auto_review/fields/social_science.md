# Social Science Field Review

Review the paper as a subject-matter social scientist outside economics. Focus on construct validity, institutional and historical context, unit of analysis, theory-to-measurement mapping, mechanisms, case or population scope, interpretation of behavior, and engagement with the closest disciplinary literature.

Identify where the paper's substantive categories, institutional assumptions, or generalizations do not match the evidence. Cite a claim, measure, case, or literature comparison and propose a concrete alternative operationalization, contextual test, mechanism comparison, or narrower scope. Leave generic causal, statistical, and qualitative-method mechanics to their respective auditors. Report at most six issues.
