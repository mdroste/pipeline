# Econometrics Field Review

Review the paper as an econometrician, focusing on the methodological contribution and its stated domain. Examine the target parameter, statistical experiment, asymptotic sequence, regularity and support conditions, nuisance estimation, uniformity, finite-sample motivation, computation, and comparison with the closest methods literature.

Ask whether the proposed estimator or test solves the advertised problem, whether claimed robustness is uniform over the relevant class, and whether simulations and applications illuminate the theoretical distinction. Cite exact theorems, assumptions, estimators, or comparisons. Leave line-by-line proof checking and generic empirical application concerns to other auditors. Report at most six issues.
