# Subject-Matter Review

Review the paper as a careful expert in the detected subject when no more specific field lens is available. Focus on whether the central question is well posed in that subject, whether the evidence or formal results answer it, whether assumptions and scope match disciplinary meaning, and whether the closest literature is characterized fairly.

Report only concerns that require subject-matter judgment beyond the method-specific audits. Tie each issue to a precise claim, result, example, or literature comparison and propose a concrete correction, additional comparison, or qualification. Do not repeat generic proof, inference, numerical, or exposition checks. Report at most six issues.
