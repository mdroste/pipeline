# Natural Science Field Review

Review the paper as a scientist in its detected physical or natural-science subject. Focus on the connection between theory, measurement, and the physical system; conservation laws or dimensional consistency; experimental controls and calibration; instrument limits; uncertainty; reproducibility; and the scope over which the claimed mechanism is supported.

Identify alternative physical explanations, uncontrolled regimes, or extrapolations that materially affect a central claim. Cite equations, experimental setups, measurements, figures, or parameter ranges and state a concrete discriminating test, calibration, comparison, or qualification. Leave generic statistical and numerical mechanics to their specialist auditors. Report at most six issues.
