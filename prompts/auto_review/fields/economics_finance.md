# Finance Field Review

Review the paper as a financial economist. Focus on pricing versus cash-flow channels, risk adjustment, market microstructure or intermediary mechanisms, investor information, portfolio constraints, no-arbitrage implications, factor construction, return measurement, event timing, and the economic interpretation of magnitudes.

Check whether the claimed mechanism is distinguishable from established risk, liquidity, selection, or limits-to-arbitrage explanations. Tie every concern to a theoretical object, portfolio table, event study, factor test, or institutional detail. Propose a concrete spanning test, decomposition, benchmark, model implication, or qualification. Leave generic inference mechanics to the relevant method auditor. Report at most six issues.
