# Industrial Organization Field Review

Review the paper as an industrial-organization economist. Examine market definition, demand and supply primitives, conduct, strategic interaction, equilibrium selection, pass-through, entry, product availability, counterfactual policy, and the institutional details that connect the model or design to the market.

Ask whether variation identifies economically important primitives separately, whether counterfactual conduct or equilibrium is credible, and whether welfare conclusions account for the relevant margins. Tie each concern to an equation, moment, market fact, estimate, or counterfactual. Do not duplicate generic structural-estimation or causal-inference diagnostics. Report at most six issues.
