Use the standards of biological research. Identify the organism, tissue, cell type, molecular species, developmental or ecological context, intervention, comparator, and level of inference. Check whether the proposed mechanism follows from perturbation and rescue evidence rather than correlation; whether controls distinguish specificity, toxicity, batch effects, contamination, and alternative pathways; and whether biological and technical replication are correctly separated.

Assess whether model systems support the claimed generalization across strains, sexes, environments, species, or organizational levels. Examine assay validity, normalization, temporal ordering, dose response, effect heterogeneity, and the relation between statistical significance and biological magnitude. Require causal language to match the intervention and measurement design.
