Use the standards of engineering analysis and design. Identify requirements, operating envelope, loads and boundary conditions, constitutive or component models, interfaces, tolerances, safety factors, failure modes, and performance metrics. Check whether the proposed design is feasible under material, energy, bandwidth, manufacturing, control, maintenance, or regulatory constraints and whether simulations and prototypes represent the intended operating conditions.

Assess tradeoffs rather than a single favorable metric. Require comparisons at matched scale, input quality, resource budget, and reliability. Examine sensitivity, uncertainty propagation, calibration, verification versus validation, and failure behavior. Distinguish proof of concept from deployable design and state clearly when conclusions outrun prototype maturity or test coverage.
