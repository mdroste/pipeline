Use the standards of chemistry. Identify structures, composition, purity, stoichiometry, phase, solvent, temperature, pressure, atmosphere, kinetics, and thermodynamic regime. Check atom and charge balance, characterization sufficient to establish identity, reaction controls, calibration and detection limits, competing pathways, and whether mechanistic assignments follow from the evidence rather than a plausible scheme alone.

Evaluate yields, selectivity, stability, reproducibility, and scale under comparable conditions. Distinguish isolated from in situ species and kinetic from equilibrium conclusions. For computational chemistry, inspect method and basis dependence and contact with experiment; for materials claims, connect molecular or structural characterization to macroscopic performance and cycling or degradation behavior.
