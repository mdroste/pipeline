Use the standards of research mathematics. Identify the exact mathematical objects, ambient category or space, regularity and finiteness assumptions, quantifier order, and scope of each main statement. Check whether definitions are well posed and consistent with standard usage; whether hypotheses are necessary, used, and preserved through reductions; and whether claimed generality survives edge cases, degenerate objects, and changes of convention.

Evaluate the conceptual advance separately from technical length. A correct proof may still provide limited novelty if it is an immediate reformulation, while a useful method may matter even with a narrow theorem. Locate the closest stated benchmark results and compare hypotheses and conclusions precisely. Flag hidden appeals to compactness, choice, measurability, differentiability, convergence, or existence when the stated setting does not supply them.
