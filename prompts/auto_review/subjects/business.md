Use the standards of management and business research. Identify the firm or market setting, decision maker, organizational process, resource and information constraints, competitive environment, performance measure, and theory of value creation or capture. Check construct validity, levels of analysis, endogenous strategy and selection, survivorship, common-method bias, and whether accounting or market measures represent the claimed organizational outcome.

Assess mechanisms, boundary conditions, implementation costs, competitive responses, and the distinction between association, prescription, and sustainable advantage. For operations and information systems, connect design choices to process constraints and reliability; for finance and accounting, connect measurement and institutional setting to incentives and valuation.
