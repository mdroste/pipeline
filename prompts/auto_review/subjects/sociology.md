Use the standards of sociology. Identify the social mechanism, actors, institutions, relational structure, unit and level of analysis, scope conditions, and historical setting. Check whether concepts are operationalized without collapsing distinct constructs; whether individual evidence supports macro claims or vice versa; and whether selection, stratification, networks, organizations, culture, and institutional context are handled consistently.

Assess competing explanations and the evidentiary link between observed patterns and the proposed mechanism. Examine category construction, reflexivity, positionality, case selection, temporal order, and generalization where relevant. Distinguish a statistically stable association from a sociologically informative explanation and a vivid case from evidence about a population or process.
