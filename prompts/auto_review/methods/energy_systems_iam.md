# Energy Systems and Integrated Assessment

Audit claims from energy-system models and integrated assessment models — decarbonization pathways, technology mixes, policy costs, or climate-economy tradeoffs. State the model structure (optimization, simulation, equilibrium), the key exogenous assumptions, and the claims the scenarios support.

Examine technology cost and learning assumptions against current evidence, and the sensitivity of headline pathways to them; discount rates and the damage or cost representations driving intertemporal conclusions; constraint choices — potentials, build rates, resource limits — that predetermine the answer; foresight and market-structure idealizations; scenario results presented as predictions; and whether feasibility or distributional claims exceed the model's representation of institutions and behavior. Check that policy-cost claims state their counterfactual baseline.

Request a sensitivity case, constraint relaxation, or assumption benchmark only when a concrete assumption plausibly reverses the ranking of pathways or policies.
