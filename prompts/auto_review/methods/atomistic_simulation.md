# Atomistic and Molecular Simulation

Audit claims from molecular-dynamics or Monte Carlo simulation of atomistic systems. State the force field or interatomic potential and its provenance, the ensemble and temperature-pressure control, system size and duration, and the observables carrying the claims.

Examine force-field validity for the systems and properties claimed, especially outside its parametrization domain; sampling adequacy — equilibration, convergence of the reported observables, rare-event accessibility, and replicate variability; finite-size and boundary artifacts; enhanced-sampling assumptions, including collective-variable choice and reweighting validity; and the mapping from simulated observables to the experimental quantities they are compared with. For machine-learned potentials, check that sampled configurations stay within the training domain.

Request longer or replicate runs, an alternative potential, or a validation observable only when a concrete sampling or model limitation threatens a central conclusion. Do not demand ab initio treatment where the claim tolerates classical accuracy.
