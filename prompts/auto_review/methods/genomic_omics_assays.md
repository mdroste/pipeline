# Genomic and Omics Assays

Audit the assay and computational pipeline from biological sampling to molecular conclusion. State the specimen and cell population, library or assay protocol, batch structure, depth and quality thresholds, reference and annotation, normalization, feature definition, and validation used for central findings.

Examine contamination, degradation, mapping and multi-mapping, duplicate handling, compositional effects, batch-confounding, cell calling and doublets, ambient signal, population structure, multiple testing, low counts, imputation, integration across cohorts or modalities, and circular annotation. For differential or pathway analyses, inspect the biological replicate, contrast, gene-set universe, and whether enrichment is driven by a few features. For single-cell work, separate cell-level abundance from donor-level evidence and check state-versus-type claims. Require orthogonal or perturbational support when the paper moves from association to mechanism.

Each issue should propose a concrete assay control, pipeline sensitivity, donor-level analysis, independent cohort, targeted validation, or narrower molecular claim.
