# Instrumental Variables

Audit the instrumental-variables design from first stage to causal interpretation. State the instrument, endogenous treatment, outcome, conditioning set, target population, and estimand. Identify the source of instrument variation and explain why it should affect the outcome only through the treatment under the paper's model.

Examine relevance and weak-instrument behavior; exclusion, independence, monotonicity, and stable-treatment assumptions; direct effects, correlated shocks, endogenous exposure shares, sorting, and treatment-effect heterogeneity. For judge, examiner, or provider designs, inspect assignment and leave-out construction. For shift-share designs, inspect shocks, shares, concentration, exposure correlation, and the appropriate source of quasi-randomness. Check many-instrument bias, clustering, finite-sample inference, and whether controls create rather than solve exclusion concerns.

Separate a valid reduced-form effect from the stronger structural or policy claim. Each comment should propose a falsification, alternative instrument construction, robust inference, bound, or narrower interpretation tied to the affected estimate.
