# Latent Variables and Psychometrics

Audit the link from observed items or indicators to the latent construct used in the paper's claims. State the construct, item population, response process, scoring model, dimensionality, scale identification, and population for which scores are interpreted.

Examine content coverage, local dependence, differential item functioning, factor or item-response assumptions, cross-loadings, method effects, reliability at relevant score levels, fit diagnostics, estimator behavior for ordinal or sparse data, and stability across groups, languages, time, and administration modes. For structural equation models, separate measurement from structural restrictions and inspect equivalent models. Check whether factor scores or sum scores carry uncertainty into downstream analyses and whether cut scores have decision-relevant validation.

High fit indices alone do not establish construct validity. Each issue should connect a measurement failure to the substantive conclusion and propose item-level diagnostics, alternative measurement structure, invariance testing, external validation, uncertainty propagation, or a narrower score interpretation.
