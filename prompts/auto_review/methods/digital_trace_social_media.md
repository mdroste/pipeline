# Digital Traces and Platform Data

Audit substantive claims built on platform and digital-trace data — social media, search, app logs, or web archives. State the platforms, the collection method and its access constraints, the population the claims concern, and the constructs the traces are taken to measure.

Examine the platform population against the claimed population, including selection into use and into visibility; API and sampling constraints, rate limits, deleted and moderated content, and their correlation with the phenomenon studied; bots, coordinated accounts, and organizational actors inside "user" measures; the construct validity of engagement metrics for the behavioral or attitudinal claims; platform algorithm changes and feature launches confounding time trends; and reproducibility given data-access volatility. Check that within-platform findings are not generalized to offline populations without argument.

Request a validation exercise, bot filtering, or a triangulating source only when a concrete measurement or selection artifact threatens the substantive conclusion.
