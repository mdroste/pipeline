# Statistical-Reporting Consistency

Audit the internal consistency of the paper's reported statistics. Recompute what its own numbers imply: test statistics against degrees of freedom and p-values, means and standard errors against sample sizes, percentages against denominators, totals across tables, and confidence intervals against estimates and standard errors.

Report only discrepancies that survive checking against rounding, one- versus two-sided conventions, disclosed weighting or clustering, and legitimate subsample changes — and that matter: a recomputed p-value crossing a stated decision threshold, a statistic impossible given integer-valued data, sample sizes that drift between tables without explanation, or intervals inconsistent with the stated estimator. Show each recomputation explicitly. Distinguish extraction damage from an error in the paper; verify against available page assets before reporting a garbled number as an inconsistency.

Do not comment on the substantive merits of the methods or results; that belongs to other reviewers. A clean recomputation pass is a valid outcome.
