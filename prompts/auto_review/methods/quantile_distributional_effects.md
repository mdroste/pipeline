# Quantile and Distributional Effects

Audit claims about effects on distributions rather than means — quantile treatment effects, distribution regression, unconditional quantile methods, or inequality decompositions. State the estimand precisely: conditional versus unconditional quantiles, quantiles of effects versus effects on quantiles, and the population contrast.

Examine rank-invariance or rank-similarity assumptions wherever individual-level statements are made; the interpretation gap between conditional quantile coefficients and the unconditional distributional claims drawn from them; quantile crossing and its repair; bunching and mass points that distort estimation at the claimed quantiles; uniform versus pointwise inference when conclusions span the distribution; and decompositions whose counterfactual reference distribution drives the headline share. Check that statements like "the effect is concentrated at the bottom" match the estimand actually computed.

Require an alternative estimand, uniform inference, or reweighting only when a concrete mismatch between claim and estimand affects a central conclusion.
