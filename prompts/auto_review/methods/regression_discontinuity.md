# Regression Discontinuity

Audit the regression-discontinuity design around the assignment cutoff. State the running variable, threshold, treatment rule, estimand, bandwidth, and population for which the effect is claimed. Determine whether the design is sharp, fuzzy, geographic, or regression-kink, and whether the reported estimand matches that design.

Check continuity of potential outcomes and predetermined covariates, manipulation or sorting, heaping and mass points, treatment compliance, functional-form and bandwidth sensitivity, support on both sides, local-polynomial order, kernel and bias correction, and inference appropriate to the effective sample. For geographic boundaries, examine compound jurisdictional changes and spatial sorting. Treat density and covariate tests as diagnostics rather than proofs of validity.

Reject extrapolation from a local cutoff effect to distant scores, populations, or policies unless the paper supplies a separate argument. Each issue should identify the affected estimate and the smallest credible diagnostic, re-estimation, bound, or scope correction.
