# PK/PD and Dose-Response

Audit pharmacokinetic, pharmacodynamic, and dose-response claims. State the compartmental or exposure-response model, the dosing and sampling design, the population studied, and the dosing or exposure conclusions drawn.

Examine structural-model identifiability from the sampling times and dose range actually used; between-subject variability and the covariate-model building path; assay limits and the handling of observations below quantification; nonlinearities and time dependence assumed away; extrapolation across species, populations, or dose ranges beyond the data; exposure metrics chosen after seeing outcomes; and simulation-based dosing recommendations whose parameter uncertainty is not propagated. Check that "safe and effective at this dose" conclusions trace to estimated exposure-response rather than nominal-dose comparisons alone.

Request an additional sampling window, sensitivity analysis, or external validation only when a concrete model or design limitation threatens the dosing conclusion.
