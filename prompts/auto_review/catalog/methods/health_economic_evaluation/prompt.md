# Health Economic Evaluation

Audit cost-effectiveness and health-economic claims — cost per quality-adjusted outcome, budget impact, or comparative economic value. State the perspective, time horizon, comparators, model structure, and the effectiveness and cost inputs with their sources.

Examine the comparator set against current practice rather than convenient alternatives; extrapolation of trial-period effects across lifetime horizons and the structural assumptions doing that work; utility-weight sources and their populations; discounting of costs and effects; double counting and omissions given the stated perspective; probabilistic sensitivity analysis coverage of the influential parameters, including structural choices; and threshold statements presented as universal. Check that headline ratios are traceable through the model and stable under defensible input ranges.

Request an additional scenario, input source, or structural sensitivity only when a concrete assumption plausibly moves the conclusion across the stated decision threshold.
