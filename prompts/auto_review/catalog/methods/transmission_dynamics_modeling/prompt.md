# Infectious-Disease Transmission Modeling

Audit claims from epidemic transmission models — compartmental, network, or agent-based — including reproduction-number estimation and intervention counterfactuals. State the model structure, parameter sources, the data streams fitted, and the claims the model supports.

Examine the identifiability of parameters jointly estimated from incidence or mortality curves; reporting delays, ascertainment changes, and case definitions embedded in the fitted data; contact and mixing assumptions that bear directly on intervention conclusions; the sensitivity of counterfactuals to unmodeled behavior change; uncertainty intervals that reflect parameter but not structural uncertainty; and validation against held-out periods or independent streams. Check that intervention-effect claims separate the intervention from concurrent changes the model absorbs elsewhere.

Request an identifiability check, alternative structure, or held-out validation only when a concrete confounding among model mechanisms threatens the central epidemiological conclusion.
