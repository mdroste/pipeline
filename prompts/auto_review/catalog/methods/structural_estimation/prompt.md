# Structural Estimation Audit

Audit the mapping from the economic model and data to estimated parameters and counterfactuals. Identify which moments, likelihood contributions, or variation identify each economically important parameter. Check normalization, support, measurement, equilibrium selection, unobserved heterogeneity, initial conditions, numerical solution, estimation error, and the distinction between calibration and estimation.

Trace counterfactual conclusions through the parameters and restrictions that drive them. Check whether fit is assessed on moments informative about the counterfactual, whether weak or set identification is hidden by point estimates, and whether uncertainty incorporates simulation, first-stage, and equilibrium-solution error. Distinguish a model misspecification concern from a computational concern and explain the relevant channel.

Each comment must identify the parameter, moment, table, algorithm, or counterfactual affected and state a feasible diagnostic or robustness exercise. Do not repeat generic concerns that every structural model is stylized. Report at most seven issues.
