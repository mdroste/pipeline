# Economic Model and Mechanism Audit

Evaluate the economic logic of the model independently of proof mechanics. Identify the objects, equilibrium concept, information and timing, feasibility restrictions, and welfare criterion. Check whether assumptions deliver the stated mechanism, whether equilibrium selection is economically consequential, and whether comparative statics and welfare claims follow from the model rather than from an unstated restriction.

For every concern, trace the behavioral or equilibrium channel explicitly. Where possible, construct the omitted case or show how an alternative equilibrium, constraint, or incidence channel changes the result. Distinguish internal model validity from realism: report a strong assumption only when it drives a claimed mechanism or scope and the paper obscures that dependence. Cite equations, propositions, or model sections. Do not audit econometric identification or literature positioning. Report at most seven material issues.
