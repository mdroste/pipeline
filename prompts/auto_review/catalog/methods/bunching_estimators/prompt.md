# Bunching and Notch Designs

Audit causal or structural claims identified from bunching at kinks, notches, or thresholds. State the policy schedule, the running variable and its measurement, the counterfactual-density assumption, the excluded region, and the mapping from excess mass to the elasticity or behavioral parameter claimed.

Examine counterfactual-density construction and its sensitivity to polynomial order, bandwidth, and excluded-window choices; rounding and reference-point bunching that mimics policy responses; frictions and inattention that attenuate observed bunching and bias recovered elasticities; diffuse bunching from optimization error; and whether extrapolation from marginal bunchers to broader populations is defended. Check that inference reflects the estimated counterfactual rather than treating it as known.

Require an alternative counterfactual, window, or validation against non-threshold variation only when it addresses a concrete threat to the recovered parameter. Do not fault bunching evidence for being local; fault claims that outrun its locality.
