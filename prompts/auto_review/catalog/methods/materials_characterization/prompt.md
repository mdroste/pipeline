# Materials Characterization

Audit whether the characterization establishes the claimed composition, structure, state, and property. Map each conclusion to the relevant technique, sample preparation, calibration, resolution, analysis model, and independent replicate. Determine what is measured at the surface, bulk, local region, or ensemble level.

Examine phase purity, impurities and contamination, preferred orientation, beam or preparation damage, instrument broadening, peak assignment and fitting, background subtraction, spatial heterogeneity, detection limits, reference standards, batch variation, and environmental or aging effects. Check whether complementary techniques rule out plausible alternative phases or mechanisms and whether ex situ measurements represent the operating state. For mechanical, thermal, electrochemical, or cycling claims, inspect protocol, geometry, normalization, conditioning, failure censoring, and uncertainty across specimens.

Each issue should identify the unsupported structure-property link and propose a reference comparison, orthogonal technique, operando measurement, fit sensitivity, replicate, mapping exercise, or narrower characterization claim.
