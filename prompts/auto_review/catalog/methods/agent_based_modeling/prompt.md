# Agent-Based Modeling

Audit conclusions drawn from agent-based or individual-based simulations. State the agents, behavioral rules, interaction structure, parameters and their sources, and which outputs carry the paper's claims.

Examine the justification for behavioral rules against the mechanisms they encode; calibration and validation targets, and whether the matched patterns are discriminating or generic; sensitivity of emergent results to seeds, initialization, updating order, network structure, and population scale; parameter sweeps that explore the space versus a single tuned trajectory; and the distinction between existence claims — the mechanism can produce the pattern — and explanatory claims that it does. Check that policy counterfactuals stay within the model's calibrated domain.

Request an additional sweep, validation target, or robustness exercise only when a concrete implementation or calibration choice plausibly drives a central emergent result. Do not fault stylization itself; fault conclusions the stylization cannot carry.
