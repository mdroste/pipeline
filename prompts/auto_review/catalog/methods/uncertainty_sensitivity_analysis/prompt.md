# Uncertainty and Sensitivity Analysis

Audit how uncertainty in inputs, assumptions, parameters, models, and scenarios reaches the claimed result. State the target output, uncertain quantities, dependence structure, ranges or distributions, propagation method, and decision or conclusion whose robustness is asserted.

Examine whether ranges are scientifically defensible, correlations and joint constraints are preserved, structural model uncertainty is represented, local derivatives are mistaken for global behavior, nonlinearities and thresholds are explored, Monte Carlo error is controlled, and worst-case combinations remain feasible. For scenario analysis, separate internally consistent scenarios from probabilistic forecasts. For robustness values or tipping points, assess whether the required departure is substantively plausible. Leave formal identified sets, sharp bounds, and inference on partially identified parameters to the dedicated partial-identification specialist.

Do not reward a large sensitivity table that omits the assumptions most capable of changing the conclusion. Each issue should identify the omitted or mis-propagated uncertainty and propose a targeted global analysis, bound, alternative model set, validation, or decision-relevant qualification.
