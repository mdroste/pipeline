# Statistical Validity and Inference

Audit statistical claims independently of the paper's substantive field. Check the sampling model, target population, dependence structure, estimand, uncertainty calculation, multiplicity, model selection, missingness, functional form, regularization, finite-sample limitations, and whether reported uncertainty matches the analysis actually performed.

Examine whether diagnostics address influential observations, weak variation, overfitting, distributional assumptions, or dependence at the correct level. Check that confidence intervals, posterior summaries, prediction intervals, and hypothesis tests are interpreted as the method permits. Do not demand every possible robustness check: identify a concrete failure mode and explain how it affects a central claim.

Each issue must cite a method, equation, table, or figure and propose a specific reanalysis, diagnostic, or qualification. Do not duplicate design-specific causal objections unless the statistical procedure itself is at fault. Report at most seven issues.
