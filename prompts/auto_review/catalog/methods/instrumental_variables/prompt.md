# Instrumental Variables

Audit the instrumental-variables design from first stage to causal interpretation. State the instrument, endogenous treatment, outcome, conditioning set, target population, and estimand. Identify the source of instrument variation and explain why it should affect the outcome only through the treatment under the paper's model.

Examine relevance and weak-instrument behavior; exclusion, independence, monotonicity, and stable-treatment assumptions; direct effects, correlated shocks, sorting, and treatment-effect heterogeneity. For judge, examiner, or provider designs, inspect assignment and leave-out construction. Check many-instrument bias, clustering, finite-sample inference, and whether controls create rather than solve exclusion concerns. Do not subsume a central shift-share exposure design into this general pass; that design requires its dedicated specialist unless a separate conventional-instrument issue is also material.

Separate a valid reduced-form effect from the stronger structural or policy claim. Each comment should propose a falsification, alternative instrument construction, robust inference, bound, or narrower interpretation tied to the affected estimate.
