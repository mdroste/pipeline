# Electronic-Structure Computation

Audit claims supported by quantum-chemical or solid-state electronic-structure calculations — density-functional, wavefunction, or many-body methods. State the method, functional or level of theory, basis set or cutoffs, pseudopotentials, and the property claims resting on the calculations.

Examine convergence with respect to basis, k-points, supercell size, and thresholds; the functional's known failure modes for the property claimed — dispersion, self-interaction, band gaps, strong correlation — and whether the choice is benchmarked for this system class; geometry provenance and whether relaxed structures correspond to the claimed phase or conformer; comparisons to experiment that mix incompatible conditions; and energy differences within the method's error bars presented as decisive. Check that computational settings are reported completely enough to reproduce the numbers.

Request a benchmark, higher-level correction, or convergence test only when a concrete method limitation threatens the central property or mechanism claim.
