# Difference-in-Differences and Event Studies

Audit the group-time comparison that identifies each causal claim. State the treated units, comparison units, adoption timing, estimand, anticipation window, and counterfactual trend assumption. For staggered adoption, determine which cohorts and periods receive weight and whether already-treated units contaminate comparisons.

Examine pre-treatment dynamics without treating insignificant pretrends as proof; differential shocks, changing composition, treatment timing, anticipation, spillovers, reversals, and outcome-specific seasonality; and inference under serial, group, and spatial dependence. Check whether fixed-effects event-study coefficients aggregate heterogeneous effects coherently and whether normalization and omitted periods support the plotted interpretation. Distinguish level breaks from trend changes and treatment effects from endogenous adoption responses.

Require an alternative comparison, estimator, diagnostic, bound, or narrower estimand only when it addresses a concrete threat to a central result. Do not ask mechanically for more leads, lags, or controls.
