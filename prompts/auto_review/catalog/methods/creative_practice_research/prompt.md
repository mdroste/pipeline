# Creative and Practice-Led Research

Audit research in which artistic or creative practice generates the central knowledge claim. Reconstruct the question, prior practice and critical context, medium and materials, process and decisions, resulting work or performance, documentation, reflective analysis, audience or participant relation, and the inferential move from practice to articulated knowledge. Check whether the submitted evidence lets a reader evaluate the process and work rather than relying on the creator's retrospective assertion alone.

Assess the relationship among experimentation, craft, interpretation, embodiment, tacit knowledge, and explicit argument; how alternatives, failures, revisions, and constraints informed the result; and whether critical reflection distinguishes discovery from intention. Evaluate claims of transfer without demanding that singular or situated practice behave like a population experiment. Distinguish research through practice from a creative output accompanied by commentary and distinguish aesthetic achievement from evidence for the paper's scholarly conclusion.

For each concern, identify the work, process decision, documentation gap, reflective inference, or scope claim affected and propose the smallest additional trace, comparative engagement, audience evidence, critical reflection, or qualification needed.
