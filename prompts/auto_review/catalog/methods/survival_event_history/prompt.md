# Survival and Event-History Analysis

Audit time-to-event and transition claims. State the origin, event definition, time scale, risk set, censoring process, competing events, recurrent-event structure, and estimand: survival, hazard, cumulative incidence, restricted mean, transition probability, or causal contrast.

Examine delayed entry, left and interval censoring, informative censoring, proportional-hazards or parametric assumptions, time-varying effects and exposures, immortal-time bias, competing risks, multi-state pathways, recurrent events, frailty, and dependence within clusters. Check whether hazard ratios are interpreted as risks or probabilities and whether noncollapsibility or depletion of susceptibles changes comparisons over time. Assess calibration and absolute risk when prognosis is claimed.

Each issue should connect a risk-set or timing error to a substantive conclusion and propose an appropriate diagnostic, alternative time scale, flexible effect, competing-risk estimand, weighting approach, or qualification. Do not request a Cox model mechanically when another estimand better answers the paper's question.
