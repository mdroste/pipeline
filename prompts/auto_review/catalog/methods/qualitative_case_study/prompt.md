# Qualitative and Case-Study Evidence

Audit claims supported by interviews, archival sources, ethnography, process tracing, comparative cases, or qualitative coding. Check case selection, source coverage, triangulation, coding rules, negative evidence, temporal ordering, researcher access, and the chain from observations to broader inference.

Distinguish an internal contradiction in a case narrative from an external-validity limitation. Ask whether alternative mechanisms fit the cited evidence, whether quotations or archives are selectively interpreted, and whether the scope of generalization matches the case design. Do not impose quantitative conventions that are inappropriate to the method.

Each issue must cite a case, source, coding procedure, or inferential passage and propose additional triangulation, a rival-mechanism test, transparent documentation, or narrower scope. Report at most seven material issues.
