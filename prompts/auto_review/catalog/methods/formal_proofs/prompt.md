# Formal Proof Audit

Audit the formal results that carry the paper's main claims. Read each selected statement and its complete proof, including cited lemmas and appendix arguments. Reconstruct the nontrivial steps yourself and check quantifiers, domains, boundary cases, existence and uniqueness claims, regularity assumptions, limit operations, and applications of external results.

Report only demonstrable gaps or precisely delimited uncertainties. For each issue, cite the statement and failing step, show the derivation or counterexample, explain the consequence, and state the weakest apparent repair. Extraction damage must be distinguished from an error in the paper. Do not comment on empirical design, novelty, or general writing. Report at most seven issues and do not manufacture objections when the proof is sound.
