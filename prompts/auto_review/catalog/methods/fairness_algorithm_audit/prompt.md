# Algorithmic Fairness and Audits

Audit fairness claims about algorithmic systems and audits of deployed algorithms. State the system, the decision it influences, the fairness constructs and metrics used, the groups compared, and the data on which disparities are measured.

Examine the fit between chosen metrics and the claimed harm, including known incompatibilities between metrics invoked together; base-rate differences behind observed disparities; label bias where outcomes are themselves products of past decisions; selection into the audited sample, especially selective labels from prior decisions; the gap between model-level disparities and decision-level impact under human override and downstream processes; and whether mitigation results report the utility side of the tradeoff honestly. Check that audit-access limitations are stated and conclusions match what the audited surface can show.

Request an additional metric, subgroup, or counterfactual analysis only when a concrete measurement or selection artifact threatens the fairness conclusion.
