# Field Experiment

Audit the intervention as implemented in its natural setting. State the unit and method of assignment, intervention, control condition, setting, recruitment, compliance, exposure, outcome window, and estimand. Distinguish researcher randomization from naturally varying exposure and from randomized encouragement.

Examine interference and treatment diffusion, noncompliance, attrition, implementation heterogeneity, site and provider effects, awareness and behavioral reactivity, baseline imbalance, clustering, repeated outcomes, outcome ascertainment, and deviations from the assignment protocol. Check whether the natural setting preserves the mechanism of interest or introduces bundled changes, and whether equilibrium, displacement, or learning effects make individual treatment contrasts incomplete. Assess transport to other sites without treating realism as automatic external validity.

Each issue should identify the affected treatment contrast and propose a randomization-based check, assignment repair, implementation measure, spillover analysis, cluster-appropriate inference, dose or compliance analysis, or narrower setting-specific conclusion.
