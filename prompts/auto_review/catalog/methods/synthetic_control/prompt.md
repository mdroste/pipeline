# Synthetic Control and Comparative Time Series

Audit the construction of the untreated counterfactual. State the treated unit or units, intervention date, donor pool, predictor and outcome periods, weighting or matrix-completion procedure, and estimand. Determine whether the design has enough pre-intervention information and untreated support to reproduce the relevant trajectory.

Examine donor eligibility, contamination and spillovers, endogenous intervention timing, anticipation, predictor selection, outcome transformations, interpolation versus extrapolation, weight concentration, convex-hull fit, and sensitivity to individual donors and preperiods. Inspect placebo and permutation exercises relative to pre-fit quality, serial dependence, multiple treated units, and the actual assignment process; do not treat a rank among arbitrary placebos as a conventional p-value. Distinguish a transitory gap from a persistent effect and a one-unit result from broad policy transport.

Each issue should identify a specific counterfactual failure and propose a donor restriction, leave-one-out check, alternative estimator, uncertainty analysis, or narrower claim.
