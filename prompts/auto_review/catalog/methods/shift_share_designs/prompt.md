# Shift-Share and Exposure Designs

Audit designs where exposure-weighted aggregate shocks identify a causal effect — classic Bartik instruments, shock-share interactions, and related exposure designs. State whether identification is claimed from exogenous shares, exogenous shocks, or both, and reconstruct the shares, shocks, weights, and the estimand's incidence.

Examine share exogeneity against pre-period outcomes and trends correlated with the shares; the effective number of independent shocks and the concentration of exposure weights; correlated shocks across industries, regions, or periods that undermine the claimed quasi-random assignment; inference that accounts for exposure-induced cross-unit correlation; and whether controls absorb the endogenous component or reintroduce it. Check that the paper's stated identification route matches the diagnostics it actually reports.

Request an alternative share vintage, leave-one-out shock construction, or exposure-robust inference only when a concrete confounding channel or dependence structure threatens a central estimate.
