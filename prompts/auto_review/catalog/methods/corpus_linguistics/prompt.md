# Corpus Linguistics

Audit linguistic claims supported by corpus evidence. State the corpus and its design, the sampling frame and registers, the operationalization of the phenomenon, and the queries or annotation pipeline that produced the counts.

Examine corpus representativeness for the claimed language, variety, period, or register; query precision and recall for the target construction, including orthographic and tokenization traps; normalization bases for frequency comparisons; dispersion versus raw frequency when generality across texts is claimed; annotation reliability for theory-laden categories; statistical treatment of non-independent tokens clustered within texts and authors; and diachronic claims confounded by changes in corpus composition. Check that absence of attestation is not converted into ungrammaticality without an argument about expected frequency.

Request an additional query validation, dispersion measure, or corpus comparison only when a concrete measurement artifact threatens the linguistic generalization.
