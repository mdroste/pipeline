# Neuroimaging Analysis

Audit claims from neuroimaging and electrophysiological data — fMRI, EEG, MEG, PET, or similar. State the design and contrasts, the preprocessing pipeline, the inference method for maps or sensors, and the brain-behavior claims drawn.

Examine circularity between region or feature selection and the statistics reported from the selected features; multiple-comparison control appropriate to the level of the claim, and the assumptions behind cluster inference; motion and physiological confounds correlated with condition or group; analytic flexibility across pipelines and whether the headline survives reasonable variants; sample sizes for individual-difference and brain-wide association claims; and reverse inference from activation to mental process. Check that connectivity and decoding claims state what those measures can and cannot support.

Request a held-out validation, alternative pipeline, or corrected inference only when a concrete analytic artifact threatens the central brain-behavior conclusion.
